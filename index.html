    The problem

    Decay has reached the pulp, the soft nerve tissue inside. It swells with nowhere to go, and that pressure is the throbbing you feel. Left alone, infection spreads to the bone at the root tip.

    Diagnosis with a 3D scan · 10 min
  2. 2 of 5

    Open and remove

    After the area is numb, a small opening on the biting surface gives access. Under 20× magnification, the inflamed pulp is lifted out of the chamber and canals.

    Local anaesthetic · 15 min
  3. 3 of 5

    Clean and shape

    Flexible nickel-titanium files shape each canal to a smooth taper while a disinfecting rinse reaches the branches files can’t.

    Rotary files + irrigation · 25 min
  4. 4 of 5

    Fill and seal

    Warm gutta-percha fills every canal right to the tip, so bacteria have no space to return. The opening is sealed the same day.

    Warm vertical fill · 20 min
  5. 5 of 5

    Crown and done

    A porcelain crown, matched to your shade, protects the treated tooth. It looks natural and you chew normally again.

    After your appointment

    What to expect at home, hour by hour.

    1. 0–2 hoursStill numb. Skip hot drinks and wait to eat until feeling returns.
    2. EveningMild tenderness when biting is normal. An over-the-counter painkiller is usually enough.
    3. Day 2–3Tenderness fades. Chew on the other side until your crown is fitted.
    4. Day 7–10Crown appointment, about 30 minutes, no injection needed for most people.
    5. 6 monthsA quick check and an X-ray to confirm the bone at the root tip has healed.

    Questions people ask before booking

    Does a root canal hurt?

    The treatment itself feels much like a filling because the tooth is fully numb. Most people come in with more pain than they leave with: removing the inflamed pulp is what stops the ache.

    Why not just pull the tooth out?

    A treated natural tooth usually lasts for decades and keeps your bite and jawbone intact. Replacing an extracted tooth with an implant or bridge costs more and takes months.

    How long does it take?

    Most molars take 60 to 90 minutes in one visit. Badly infected teeth sometimes need a second short visit with a medicated dressing in between.

    Do I really need a crown afterwards?

    Back teeth take heavy biting forces and become more brittle after treatment, so a crown is strongly recommended. Front teeth often only need a bonded filling.

    How much will it cost?

    You’ll get a written estimate after your 3D scan and before any treatment starts. Treatment and crown are priced separately so you can plan.

    Can I drive or go back to work the same day?

    Yes. Local anaesthetic doesn’t affect driving, and most people go straight back to work.
